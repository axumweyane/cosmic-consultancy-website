# Use cases for Cosmic Consultancy

Illustrative scenarios (NOT case studies — no named clients, no past-result claims).
These show prospects how each service solves a real problem. Safe, honest, competitive.
2 per service. Claude Code can place these as a "Common use cases" block on each service page.

---

## Cloud Technologies
- **Peak-season scaling** — A retailer whose site buckles every Black Friday moves to auto-scaling cloud infrastructure: capacity ramps up for the rush and back down after, so they handle the spike without paying for idle servers the rest of the year.
- **Legacy cost rescue** — A company bleeding money on an over-provisioned data center migrates and right-sizes its workloads, cutting idle spend and gaining the elasticity its old setup never had.

## IT Strategy & Consulting
- **Post-merger consolidation** — Two merged companies running duplicate, incompatible systems get a single IT roadmap that consolidates tooling, removes redundancy, and sets a clear modernization path.
- **Tech-debt triage** — A startup whose quick-build stack now slows every release gets an honest architecture assessment that prioritizes what to refactor, replace, or leave alone.

## Cyber Security
- **Compliance fast-track** — A SaaS company that needs SOC 2 to close enterprise deals gets a gap assessment, implemented controls, and full documentation so it passes audit and unlocks bigger contracts.
- **Ransomware resilience** — A firm rattled by an industry breach hardens endpoints, segments its network, and puts tested backup-and-recovery in place so an attack can't halt the business.

## AI Integration
- **Support automation** — A support team drowning in repetitive tickets deploys an AI assistant that resolves routine queries and routes the rest, freeing agents for the complex cases that need a human.
- **Decision intelligence** — A logistics company that guesses at demand integrates predictive models into planning, replacing gut-feel forecasts with data-driven ones that cut overstock and stockouts.

## Data Analytics & BI
- **Single source of truth** — Leadership pulling conflicting numbers from five tools gets a unified data warehouse and executive dashboard, so every decision starts from the same trusted figures.
- **Real-time ops visibility** — A manufacturer that only spots problems at month-end pipes live operational data into dashboards, surfacing issues the moment they happen.

## CRM Integration
- **Unified customer view** — Sales and support working from different systems get their CRM connected end to end, so every team sees the same real-time customer history.
- **Contact-center sync** — Agents who waste the first minute of every call hunting for account info get the contact center wired to the CRM, so customer data appears the instant a call connects.

## ERP Solutions
- **Spreadsheet escape** — A growing company running finance and inventory on fragile spreadsheets moves to an ERP that ties finance, supply chain, and operations into one system that scales.
- **Multi-location rollup** — A business spread across several locations gets its ERP integrated site-to-site, so reporting, inventory, and finance roll up in real time instead of by hand.

## App & Web Development
- **Legacy app replacement** — A slow, crash-prone core internal app is rebuilt on a modern full-stack foundation: faster, mobile-friendly, and maintainable.
- **MVP to market** — A founder who needs to validate an idea fast gets a production-quality MVP ready for real users, with foundations that won't need a rewrite when it scales.

## Quality Assurance / Testing
- **Release confidence** — A team that ships often but keeps hitting production bugs gets an automated test suite that catches regressions before release, not after customers do.
- **Pre-launch hardening** — A company about to launch a high-stakes product has it stress-tested across devices and edge cases, so launch day goes smoothly.
