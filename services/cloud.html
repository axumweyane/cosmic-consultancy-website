Cloud Technologies

Cloud migration, cost optimization, and managed cloud on AWS, Azure & Google Cloud — auto-scaling infrastructure that handles peak demand and cuts idle spend.

What we deliver

Our Cloud Technologies practice is delivered by senior engineers and owned end to end — no handoffs, no diluted accountability. Here's what working with us looks like.

Capabilities

  • Cloud migration strategy and execution (AWS, Azure, GCP)
  • Cost optimization and right-sizing
  • Managed cloud operations and 24/7 monitoring
  • High-availability and disaster-recovery design
  • Containerization and Kubernetes orchestration

How we engage

Every engagement starts with a free consultation, moves through a scoped design with clear milestones, and ends in production-grade delivery with ongoing support. You always know what's happening and why.

Whether you're moving your first workloads to the cloud or reining in an over-provisioned environment, we focus on outcomes that show up on both the invoice and the status page — lower monthly spend, higher uptime, and infrastructure that scales without surprises. With most enterprise workloads now running in the cloud, we make sure yours do so efficiently and securely.

Common use cases

Illustrative examples of how this service solves real problems.

Peak-season scaling

A retailer whose site buckles every Black Friday moves to auto-scaling cloud infrastructure: capacity ramps up for the rush and back down after, so they handle the spike without paying for idle servers the rest of the year.

Legacy cost rescue

A company bleeding money on an over-provisioned data center migrates and right-sizes its workloads, cutting idle spend and gaining the elasticity its old setup never had.
